How it works

From box to smart home in three steps

  1. 01

    Download & create your nest

    Install NovaNest on iOS or Android, create a home profile, and invite family members in one tap.

  2. 02

    Connect your devices

    Scan, search, or auto-detect compatible lights, thermostats, locks, and cameras — no wiring required.

  3. 03

    Set scenes & forget

    Choose ready-made routines like Movie Night or Good Morning, or build your own with a simple drag-and-drop editor.

About NovaNest

We believe smart homes should feel human

Founded in London in 2021, NovaNest Technologies Ltd. builds software that makes connected living accessible, affordable, and genuinely easy — for families and homeowners who don't want to become IT experts.

Our mission is simple: put powerful automation in everyone's hands, with design that feels warm and intuitive, not cold and corporate. We're growing across Europe so more homes can enjoy the same effortless experience.
